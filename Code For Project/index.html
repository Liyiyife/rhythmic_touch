Study instructions

What you will do

You will complete two practice tasks followed by three study blocks. Practice results are not included in the study data.

1

Feedback modality

Four fixed conditions: no feedback, visual feedback, audio feedback, and haptic feedback.

2

Rhythmic complexity

Two fixed conditions: simple rhythm and complex rhythm.

3

Input action type

Two fixed conditions: tap-only and tap-and-swipe.

The order of the three study blocks is counterbalanced by participant number. You will complete a short rating after each condition and six NASA-TLX ratings after each block.

Practice

Learn the controls

Preview each pattern, then reproduce it in the input area. Practice results are not saved as study data.

Tap: briefly touch the input area.

Swipe: slide your finger to the right.

Retry: if the pattern is not recognised, wait for the input area to reset and try again.
